⌚ Admin Panel Guide

Media Upload & Inventory Management

📸 Media Upload (Photos & Videos)

Upload showcase images and videos directly from your admin panel. These appear in the store's media gallery.

1
Open Admin Panel

Go to admin-master.html

2
Click "Media" Section

In the sidebar, click the 📸 Media button

3
Upload Photos

Two ways:

  • Drag & drop photos into the "Showcase Photos" area
  • Click the area to open file picker

Supports: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF

4
Upload Videos

Same process for videos:

  • Drag & drop videos into the "Showcase Videos" area
  • Or click to browse

Supports: MP4, WebM, OGG

5
View Previews

After upload, thumbnails appear instantly. Click the ✕ button to remove any media.

✓ Media appears in store
Open store.html → scroll down → see your gallery in "Brand Gallery" section
⚠ Storage Limit
Browser storage: ~5-10MB total. For unlimited storage, set up Imgur (see below).

⌚ Inventory Management (Products)

Manage your watch inventory directly from the admin panel. Add, edit, and delete products with real-time store updates.

1
Click "Products" Section

In sidebar, click ⌚ Products

2
Click "+ Add Product" Button

A modal form will open

3
Fill Product Details
  • Product Name: e.g., "Submariner"
  • Brand: Select from dropdown (add brands first in Brands section)
  • Price: e.g., "9500"
  • Gender: Mens / Womens / Unisex
  • Stock: Number in inventory
4
Click "Add Product"

Product appears in the table below and in your store instantly

✓ Product is live
Open store.html → select the brand tab → see your product in the grid

🔍 Search

In the store, customers can search your products by name

🏷️ Filter by Brand

Tabs show your products organized by brand

👥 Gender Filter

Customers can filter by Mens / Womens / Unisex

🛒 Add to Cart

Customers add products to cart, which persists in their browser

🖼️ Image Hosting Options

Choose how to store your media based on your needs.

📱 Browser Storage (Default)

  • No setup needed
  • Works offline
  • Limit: 5-10MB
  • Per device/browser
  • Demo/testing

☁️ Imgur (Recommended)

  • Free account
  • Unlimited storage
  • Permanent links
  • Shared across devices
  • 5-minute setup

Setup Imgur (5 minutes)

1
Create Imgur Account

Go to https://imgur.com/register

2
Create API Client

Go to https://imgur.com/oauth2/addclient

Choose: "Anonymous use" (no callback URL)

3
Copy Your Client-ID

You'll see a Client-ID like: abcd1234efgh5678

4
Add to Admin Panel

Open admin-master.html in a text editor

Find line ~670:

const IMGUR_CLIENT_ID = '';

Paste your Client-ID between the quotes:

const IMGUR_CLIENT_ID = 'abcd1234efgh5678';
5
Save and Reload

Save the file, reload admin panel in browser

Now upload media → it goes to Imgur (permanent links!)

✓ Unlimited media storage
No more storage limits. Media links work forever.

💡 Tips & Best Practices

📸 Photo Tips
  • Use high-quality images (2000x2000px or larger)
  • Compress before uploading (use tools like TinyPNG)
  • Show multiple angles of each watch
  • Include product shots and lifestyle photos
🎬 Video Tips
  • Keep videos under 30 seconds
  • Show the watch in action
  • Use MP4 format (most compatible)
  • Compress video files (use tools like Handbrake)
⌚ Product Tips
  • Use clear, descriptive product names
  • Set accurate pricing
  • Keep stock numbers up to date
  • Use correct gender classification

🔧 Troubleshooting

❌ Media won't upload
  • File too large? Compress it first
  • Wrong format? Use JPG/PNG for images, MP4 for videos
  • Storage full? Clear browser data (Settings → Storage)
❌ Changes don't show in store
  • Make sure you clicked "Save" (for policies, company info)
  • Refresh the store page (F5)
  • Check browser console for errors (F12)
❌ Can't see uploaded media in store
  • Scroll down to "Brand Gallery" section
  • Make sure media uploaded successfully (check preview thumbnails)
  • Try clearing browser cache
